# Fictional electricity-reporting procedure

DEMO ONLY. This example is invented for a portfolio project and is not Vinarchy policy.

Purpose: collect a consistent monthly electricity report for human review.

1. The site coordinator records the site name, reporting period start date and reporting period end date.
2. The site coordinator records electricity usage in kWh and identifies the reporting owner.
3. The site coordinator attaches a meter reading or supplier bill covering the reporting period.
4. The reporting owner checks that the end date is on or after the start date and that electricity usage is non-negative.
5. If information or evidence is missing, the reporting owner returns the report to the site coordinator for correction.
6. A reviewer checks the evidence against the reported figures and records their decision.
7. Only after human approval may the reporting owner submit the report through the organisation's normal reporting process.
