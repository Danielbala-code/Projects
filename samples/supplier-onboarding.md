# Fictional supplier-onboarding procedure

DEMO ONLY. This is invented sample data, not an organisation's purchasing policy.

Purpose: prepare a supplier record for human review.

1. The purchasing coordinator records the supplier's legal name, contact email and invoice currency.
2. The purchasing coordinator attaches the signed onboarding form and sends the record to the reviewer.
3. The reviewer checks the form and approves the supplier record before any order is placed.
