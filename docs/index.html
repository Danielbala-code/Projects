02 / MAKE THE MODEL EARN ITS PLACE

A simple rule is the benchmark.

Fixed before evaluation
A

Training prevalence

Assign everyone July's repeat-purchase rate. Establishes a probability baseline with no personalisation.

B

Recency rule

Rank customers by how recently they purchased. A fast, inspectable rule; its score is not a probability.

C

Logistic regression

Learn from recency, purchase count, positive purchase value and observed tenure. Small CPU model with visible coefficients.

Separate the past, prediction date and answer

Each cohort includes known customers with at least one positive purchase in the preceding 90 days. The same customers may appear at different dates. No prediction is evaluated without a complete 30-day outcome window.

Measured model comparison

ROC-AUC: ranking quality across purchasers and non-purchasers; 0.5 is chance ranking.

Average precision: precision across ranked thresholds; compare with the cohort's repeat rate.

Top 20% precision: share of the highest-ranked fifth who actually purchased again. This is observed association, not outreach impact.

Identical scores break ties by private customer ID. The prevalence baseline's top-fifth result is an arbitrary ordering. No hyperparameters or thresholds were tuned on the holdout.

What the learned model uses

Coefficients are per training standard deviation, with other inputs held fixed. Association is not cause. Count and value use log(1 + x). The scaler fits July only.

No embeddings or retrieval needed: these are numeric purchase histories. Qwen is a separate prose assistant, not the predictor.

Check probabilities, not just ranking

Brier score is mean squared probability error; lower is better. These probabilities are not certified for production. Changes in seasonal purchasing can affect calibration.

03 / PREPARE A CONTROLLED EXPERIMENT

Eligibility comes before a score.

Separate fictional cases

The retail source has no marketing consent. This isolated fixture tests ownership, consent, duplicate identity and a 14-day cooldown. These are invented people and territories, with no connection to real source customers.

Test / control allocation

Export follows the selected territory. Stable hash ordering alternates groups within territory and segment. No campaign is sent or uplift measured. Production assignments must be persisted.

Exceptions that block outreach

Both copies of a duplicate identity are excluded. Future/recent contact dates also block eligibility.

A high purchase score is not permission to contact someone, and it does not show who would benefit from a campaign.
